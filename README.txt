ALICE Format Generator — Ice Age and Alliances

Open index.html in a browser with internet access. Scryfall supplies set-specific cards and images.
Ice Age Starter: 3 rares, 9 uncommons, 26 commons, 22 lands (normal and snow-covered).
Ice Age Booster: 1 rare, 3 uncommons, 11 commons.
Alliances Booster: 12 cards, modeled as 1 rare, 3 uncommon, 8 common by Scryfall rarity.
NOTE: Alliances historically used unusual print sheets; this version approximates the slot distribution and does NOT exactly reproduce print-sheet odds.
Cards are not generated until a product is clicked.
